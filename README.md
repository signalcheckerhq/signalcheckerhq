# SignalCheckerHQ

Crypto trading tools that check claims against real exchange prices, with fees and losses counted.

- **Free calculators** (no sign-up): [liquidation price](https://signalcheckerhq.org/liquidation-calculator/), [position size](https://signalcheckerhq.org/position-size-calculator/), [break-even win rate](https://signalcheckerhq.org/break-even-win-rate-calculator/), [DCA](https://signalcheckerhq.org/dca-calculator/) and [more](https://signalcheckerhq.org/tools/)
- **Research:** [3,181 signal calls from six channels and accounts, replayed with fees](https://signalcheckerhq.org/guides/crypto-signal-channel-statistics/) · [how we count](https://signalcheckerhq.org/how-we-count/)
- **Apps:** [Signal Checker, Trade Journal + DCA Planner, Portfolio Tracker](https://signalcheckerhq.org/pricing/): free plan, phone and computer, offline
- **Bot Lab:** free grid & DCA bot simulator on real prices · **Bot Lab Pro** adds futures, 4 exchanges and paper bots
- In English, Spanish and Russian

**Open source here:** [tradingview-scripts](https://github.com/signalcheckerhq/tradingview-scripts) (Pine Script v6: real results after fees, bot profit vs real result, liquidation vs stop) · [prop-firm-tools](https://github.com/signalcheckerhq/prop-firm-tools) (read-only prop-firm rule indicators for NinjaTrader 8 and MetaTrader 5)
